# Water occurrence

How often surface water was present, March 1984 to December 2024, as a percentage of valid observations. From the [GSW v1.5 Data Users Guide](https://storage.googleapis.com/water-world/downloads_ancillary/DataUsersGuidev2024_v.5.pdf): "The frequency with which water was present on the surface from March 1984 to December 2024 was captured in a single product called surface water occurrence (WO). To compute WO, the water detections (WD) and valid observations (VO) from the same months are summed [...] such that WOmonth = ∑WD month / ∑VO month. Averaging the results of all monthly WOmonth calculations gives the long-term overall surface water occurrence." The collection holds the 504 upstream 10° × 10° tiles as items, copied byte for byte (0.00025° grid, EPSG:4326, uint8, nodata 255), and one global overview COG at 0.004° for display. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for access patterns.

## Known Issues

The Data Users Guide lists two bands for occurrence change intensity (`change_abs` and `change_norm`); the downloadable tiles hold one band, which matches the `change_norm` value table. The guide states that seasonality covers only 2022 to 2024 and that 2022 onward uses Landsat Collection 2, which "introduces a residual co-registration offset" of up to about one 30 m pixel in some path/rows. Monthly history, yearly history, and monthly recurrence are not in this mirror.
