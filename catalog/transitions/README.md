# Water transitions

Change in seasonality between the first and last year of observation: ten classes of water transition. From the [GSW v1.5 Data Users Guide](https://storage.googleapis.com/water-world/downloads_ancillary/DataUsersGuidev2024_v.5.pdf): "The Transitions map provides information on the change in seasonality between the first and last years and captures changes between the three classes of not water, seasonal water and permanent water. [...] These conversions refer to changes in state from the beginning and end of the time series; they do not describe what happened in the intervening years." The collection holds the 504 upstream 10° × 10° tiles as items, copied byte for byte (0.00025° grid, EPSG:4326, uint8, nodata 255), and one global overview COG at 0.004° for display. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for access patterns.

## Known Issues

The Data Users Guide lists two bands for occurrence change intensity (`change_abs` and `change_norm`); the downloadable tiles hold one band, which matches the `change_norm` value table. The guide states that seasonality covers only 2022 to 2024 and that 2022 onward uses Landsat Collection 2, which "introduces a residual co-registration offset" of up to about one 30 m pixel in some path/rows. Monthly history, yearly history, and monthly recurrence are not in this mirror.
