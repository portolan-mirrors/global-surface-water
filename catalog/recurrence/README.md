# Water recurrence

How often water returned from year to year, as a percentage of observation years within each pixel's water period. From the [GSW v1.5 Data Users Guide](https://storage.googleapis.com/water-world/downloads_ancillary/DataUsersGuidev2024_v.5.pdf): "Water recurrence is a measurement of the degree of inter-annual variability in the presence of water. This describes how frequently water returned from one year to another (expressed as a percentage). [...] Water recurrence is then calculated as the ratio of the number of water years to observation years." The collection holds the 504 upstream 10° × 10° tiles as items, copied byte for byte (0.00025° grid, EPSG:4326, uint8, nodata 255), and one global overview COG at 0.004° for display. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for access patterns.

## Known Issues

The Data Users Guide lists two bands for occurrence change intensity (`change_abs` and `change_norm`); the downloadable tiles hold one band, which matches the `change_norm` value table. The guide states that seasonality covers only 2022 to 2024 and that 2022 onward uses Landsat Collection 2, which "introduces a residual co-registration offset" of up to about one 30 m pixel in some path/rows. Monthly history, yearly history, and monthly recurrence are not in this mirror.
